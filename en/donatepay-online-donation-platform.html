Frequently Asked Questions

Can DonatePay support recurring donations?

Yes. DonatePay supports one-off and recurring donations.

Can donation receipts be automated?

Yes. The platform supports automated email notifications and donation receipts.

Can donors create their own fundraising pages?

Yes. DonatePay can enable donors to register as fundraisers and create personal fundraising pages.

Does the platform support multiple languages?

Yes. DonatePay supports multilingual content, and the donation experience is mobile responsive.

What is the difference between Standard and Premium Plan?

The Standard Plan provides a simple and cost-effective donation solution with essential donation, receipting, donor management and reporting functions. The Premium Plan adds campaign and event management, personal fundraiser pages, social sharing, donor interaction tools, and advanced dashboards and analytics.

Which payment methods are supported in Hong Kong?

DonatePay supports popular Hong Kong payment methods including Visa, Mastercard, AlipayHK, PayMe, FPS, Octopus, and more. Availability may depend on the merchant setup and service configuration.
