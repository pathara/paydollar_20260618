SoftPos

Transforms everyday devices into secure payment tools.📱

What is SoftPos?

Tired of deciding? SoftPos is here to help. It's an innovative payment solution designed to help you accept digital and card payments securely and conveniently. Whether in-store or on-the-go, SoftPos will make sure you can accept payments anytime anywhere. No machine, no stress, just your phone.

Key Features

Everything you need, built right into your smartphone

Contactless tap to pay

Accepts AMEX, Diners Club, Discover and digital wallets in one simple tap.

Consumer-presented

Customers display their QR codes on their devices, and the merchant scans to collect.

Merchant-presented

Merchants generate a QR code and present it to the customer for scanning.

AppPay Terminal Code

Customers scan the terminal code or QR to make payments on PayDollar.

Dashboard

Access to real-time sales monitoring and transaction history analytics.

Digital receipts

Receipts of successful payments can be instantly emailed to customers.

Accept payment voids

Merchants have full control and can easily null or void a payment when necessary.

Multi-lingual options

Our system is highly adaptable and is available in multiple languages.

Security

Our system is robust, backed by PCI-DSS Level 1 certified tokenization technology.

Drive Sales & Revenue

Built on secure firmware, fully compliant with international payment security standards.
